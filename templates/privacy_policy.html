Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026 • PPM Organic Farms

1 Introduction

Welcome to PPM Organic Farms ("we", "our", or "us"). We are dedicated to protecting your personal privacy and ensuring that your farm-to-table shopping experience is transparent, secure, and respectful of your data. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application and web portal collect, use, and safeguard your information.

2 Information We Collect

To process and deliver your fresh organic harvest orders, we collect the following minimal necessary information:

  • Account Credentials: Full name and unique 10-digit mobile phone number for account login and SMS OTP verification.
  • Delivery Details: Physical delivery addresses (street name, apartment, landmarks) to dispatch fresh produce to your doorstep.
  • Order Transactions: Produce items purchased, order totals, Cash on Delivery (COD) status, and reward coin redemptions.
  • Support Interactions: Queries sent through the 24/7 Kisan AI assistant or our WhatsApp support line (+91 76759 60440).

3 How We Use Your Data

We use the collected information strictly for legitimate commercial and operational purposes:

  • Dispatching and delivering farm-fresh vegetables to your selected location.
  • Verifying cash-on-delivery payments upon parcel handover.
  • Sending real-time order status updates and dispatch notifications.
  • Calculating personalized produce recommendations and restock alerts.
  • Managing loyalty cashback coins and customer support requests.

4 Data Sharing & Third Parties

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to advertisers or third parties. Data is shared only with direct logistics delivery personnel strictly to complete the physical delivery of your order.

5 Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have complete control over your information. You can view, add, or delete saved delivery addresses at any time through your dashboard. If you wish to delete your account and associated order records, you can contact us at any time via WhatsApp or phone at +91 76759 60440.
